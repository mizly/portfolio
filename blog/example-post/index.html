Building small things in public

August 24, 2026 · 2 min read

I like projects that begin a little too small to be impressive: a strange prototype, a weekend experiment, or a tool that solves one oddly specific problem.

Small projects leave room for curiosity. They make it easier to change direction, share unfinished work, and notice the details that would get lost in a larger plan. Some become useful. Some become stories. Both are good outcomes.

Make room for the next idea

My goal is to keep building things that sit somewhere between technology and art. The best way I know to do that is to stay interested, publish what I can, and let each experiment point toward the next one.
